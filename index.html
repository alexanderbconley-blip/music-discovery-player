AI Music Discovery Player

Describe the music you're looking for. AI recommends titles and artists; the player finds recordings on YouTube or Spotify as you move through the recommendations.

AI Discovery Prompts

Add as many prompts as you wish. Each prompt keeps its own recommendations and playback history. Select Explore once to discover and start the first song; select Explore again on a locked prompt to add more recommendations without interrupting playback. Expand a recommendation list to select a song, or drag over its title and artist to highlight and copy that one recommendation.

Prompts are saved in this browser. Drag the handle or use the arrows to change their order.

Connection setup

Secure and deploy your Cloudflare Worker before using AI Discovery.

The Worker URL is not an API secret. Do not enter an OpenAI API key here. This page sends an AI request when you first Explore a prompt or click Explore again to request more songs.

Enter the private value you created for PLAYER_ACCESS_KEY in Cloudflare, not the words PLAYER_ACCESS_KEY. The key is sent only to your Cloudflare Worker when requesting new AI recommendations. It is not saved in browser history, exports, or GitHub. Reenter it if you reload the page or return from Spotify sign-in.

Your YouTube key is sent to Google for searches; it isn't saved in this player's history. Each YouTube search uses API quota.

Discovery History & Backup

Your prompts, recommendations, played checkmarks, and saved recording matches are saved automatically in this browser. Export a backup to keep a copy or move it to another browser.

Explore creates the first recommendations and attempts to play the first song automatically. Click Explore again on a locked prompt to append new, nonduplicate recommendations without interrupting playback. Your browser may require a Play click to allow sound. Selecting a recommendation searches for that recording when first reached, or replays a previously found recording without searching again. When a recording finishes, the player advances to the next recommendation or the next unexplored prompt. Previous, Next, and Stop also work manually. A gray checkmark means playback started here or you opened the recording directly on YouTube to listen. Search status is displayed separately for each song, and a possible match may not be the exact requested version.
